The Problem

Right now, class happens under a tin roof — or no roof at all.

Three of our existing structures are temporary tin-sheet rooms built in 2011. In summer, indoor temperatures cross 40°C. In monsoon, classes are cancelled when it rains. Four sections currently share two rooms in shifts, cutting real teaching time in half.

Peak summer classroom temperatures regularly exceed safe learning conditions.

Classes cancelled in monsoon

Leaking roofs mean an average of 23 lost teaching days every year.

Two rooms, four sections, in shifts

Overcrowding forces a split-shift timetable that halves real instruction time.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Is my donation tax-exempt? ▾
Yes. Sanvi Educational and Charitable Trust is registered under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act. You'll receive an instant email receipt valid for tax deduction within 24 hours of donating.
How do I know the money is actually used for this project? ▾
Funds raised for this campaign are held in a separate project account, audited annually by an independent CA, and a public utilization report is published every quarter on this page.
Can I pay via UPI or bank transfer instead of card? ▾
Yes — select "Bank Transfer / UPI" at checkout and our system will show our account and UPI QR code, or email donate@sanvitrust.org for assisted giving.
When will the 10 classrooms be completed? ▾
Construction is phased by funding. At current pace we expect the first 4 classrooms ready in 6 months and all 10 within 14 months of reaching the full goal.
Can my company match this donation through CSR? ▾
Yes, this project qualifies under Schedule VII of the Companies Act for CSR spend on education. Select "Corporate / CSR" above and our team will send a formal proposal and MOU.
