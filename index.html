The Problem

Right now, inclusion stops at the door, because there isn't enough room.

Sanvi already runs Inclusive Education, Early Intervention, Free Education for paternal orphans, and a Day Care Centre, all out of one small facility in Puducherry. Classes, therapy sessions, and assessments currently share the same handful of rooms. Every new child enrolled in one programme means less room for another.

Therapy and classes share one room

Early Intervention assessments and Inclusive Education classes currently compete for the same limited space.

Children are wait-listed

Families seeking an inclusive seat for their child are turned away each term for lack of room.

One roof, four programmes

Inclusive Education, Free Education, Day Care, and Talent Identification all operate from the same small building.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Is my donation tax-exempt? ▾
Yes. Sanvi Educational and Charitable Trust is registered under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act. You'll receive an instant email receipt valid for tax deduction within 24 hours of donating.
How do I know the money is actually used for this project? ▾
Funds raised for this campaign are held in a separate project account, audited annually by an independent CA, and a public utilization report is published every quarter on this page.
Can I pay via UPI or bank transfer instead of card? ▾
Yes, select "Bank Transfer / UPI" at checkout and our system will show our account and UPI QR code, or email director@sectpondy.org for assisted giving.
When will the 10 classrooms be completed? ▾
Construction is phased by funding. At current pace we expect the first 4 classrooms ready in 6 months and all 10 within 14 months of reaching the full goal.
Can my company match this donation through CSR? ▾
Yes, this project qualifies under Schedule VII of the Companies Act, which lists special education for the differently abled among its eligible CSR categories. Select "Corporate / CSR" above and our team will send a formal proposal and MOU.
